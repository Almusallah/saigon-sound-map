🤖 Android

Download the app file and open it. Android will ask you to allow installing from the browser — that's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

Download for Android (.apk)

If nothing happens after downloading: open your Downloads, tap the file, and allow the install when asked.

🍎 iPhone / iPad

Apple only allows link-installed apps through Safari:

  1. Open the map in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen

You get the same full app — icon, full screen, recording, photos. This is Apple's official way to install web apps; nothing is missing compared to the Android file.
